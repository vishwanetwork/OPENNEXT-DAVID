# Partner and program artwork

The product owner confirmed the relationships and supplied the three program
names on 2026-11-01. On 2026-11-02 the owner requested that only Fidelity Labs
remain in the partner group and provided its official LinkedIn page as the logo
reference. The obsolete atom mark and the Pharos, Ondo and Solana assets were
removed. Programs remain a separate group; no security certification or
investment claim is added.

Files are hosted locally so loading the page does not request third-party images.
Artwork is sourced, not redrawn. Fidelity Labs retains the green mark shown on
its LinkedIn page, without recoloring, cropping or distortion. The adjacent
company name is ordinary page text. The program logos retain their monochrome
display treatment.

The owner also requested a separate "Powered by Vishwa Lab" attribution on
2026-11-02. Its original transparent white wordmark comes from the header of
Vishwa's official website and is hosted locally without editing the image.
CSS displays it in black on the white hero strip. It is separate from the partner
and program groups, and links to `https://vishwalab.com/`.

Brand ownership remains with the respective organizations; public availability of
an asset is not a grant of trademark rights. In particular, Anthropic's published
guidelines require approved use of its marks; the release owner should use the
CVP-provided permission/materials for public presentation.
